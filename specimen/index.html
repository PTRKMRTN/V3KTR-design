Home/Looks/Depth Parallax

How depth changes a filter.

A flat filter treats every pixel the same. A depth-aware one knows the foreground from the sky.

Why depth matters

Most image effects work on colour alone. Processor also knows how far away each part of the picture is, so a blur can fall off with distance and a glow can sit behind a subject instead of on top of it.

What you need

  • A browser with WebGPU.
  • One photo. The depth model downloads once, about 50 MB.
The image seeds the structure, not just the palette.

Settings travel with the export: open a PNG and the whole stack comes back. See the technical notes or run read-fx-meta.
